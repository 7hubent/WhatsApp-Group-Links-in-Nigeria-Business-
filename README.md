# Types of WhatsApp Groups
WhatsApp groups come in various types, each serving different purposes and catering to diverse needs. Here are some common types of WhatsApp groups:

Social Groups
These groups are formed among friends, family members, or colleagues to stay connected, share updates, and plan gatherings or events.

Interest-based Groups
These groups revolve around specific interests or hobbies, such as sports, gaming, cooking, music, or photography. Members bond over their shared passions and exchange relevant content and tips.

Educational Groups
These groups are created for learning and knowledge-sharing purposes. They can include study groups, language exchange groups, or professional development groups where members discuss educational resources, ask questions, and seek assistance.

Business or Work-related Groups
These groups are commonly used by organizations, companies, or teams to facilitate communication, collaboration, and coordination on work-related matters. They can be used for project discussions, task assignments, and sharing updates.

Community or Support Groups
These groups provide a platform for individuals facing similar challenges or sharing common experiences to connect, seek advice, and offer support to one another. They can cover a wide range of topics, such as mental health support groups, parenting groups, or support groups for specific health conditions.



# Networking Groups
These groups are designed to facilitate networking and professional connections. They can be industry-specific networking groups, job search groups, or entrepreneurship groups where members share opportunities, exchange ideas, and build professional relationships.

Entertainment Groups
These groups are created for sharing entertaining content such as memes, jokes, funny videos, or movie recommendations. They serve as a source of amusement and relaxation for members.

Religious or Cultural Groups
These groups bring together individuals who share the same religious beliefs or cultural background. Members discuss religious teachings, traditions, and practices, as well as share relevant events and celebrations.

Creating Group Links
Step-by-Step Guide

Launch WhatsApp on your smartphone and navigate to the desired group you want to create a link for.
Once inside the group chat, tap on the group name at the top to access the Group Info or Settings menu.
Within the Group Info or Settings menu, scroll down until you find the “Invite to Group via Link” option.


# Group Link Settings
Choose whether you want to invite users via link or QR code. Select “Invite via Link” to generate a shareable link or “Invite via QR Code” to display a QR code that others can scan to join.
Set group invitation permissions according to your preferences. You can decide whether anyone with the link can join the group directly or if members need to be approved by an admin before joining.
Consider setting an expiry date for the group link or the ability to revoke the link at any time to control access to the group and maintain security and privacy. This feature is particularly useful for Pakistani groups where maintaining control over membership is crucial for safety and relevance.
